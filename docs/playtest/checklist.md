## Measured block — exact facilitator prompts

1. **0–20: silent play.** Two rooms ≤8 min each; ≤4 min neutral checks/transitions. “We’re testing the game, not you. Play normally without explaining thoughts; spontaneous reactions fine. Voice/chat for technical/access problems, breaks, hints or stopping only. No puzzle advice. Stop anytime; pay unchanged.” No why/aha prompts/coaching.
2. **Each room.** Start board-ready/running; note video offset. Restarts retain time. At **300 active s**: “Keep trying, see the first hint, move on, or stop—which do you prefer?” Unaided window ends. Offers 360/420 s, ≥60 s after prior hint; cap **480 s**. Early hints assisted; never force. Distress stops immediately.
3. **20–23: free choice.** “Required play finished. Replay, another room, free play, or finish—where available. Equally useful, pay unchanged.” Enacted = input + ≥60 s or earlier clear; sandbox separate.
4. **23–31: questionnaire/transfer.** Rule + novel prediction, aha dimensions, enjoyment/competence. **Autonomy not assessed in lean.** No corrections; flag learning from optional replay.
5. **31–37: cued retrospective.** ≤2 clips. “What do you remember noticing? What did you expect next?” Allow “I don’t remember.” Aha ≠ correctness.
6. **37–40: debrief/save.** “Anything unclear or difficult?” “May Finish study download one local JSON? Nothing uploads. Send only if you still consent.” Auto-download with prior consent; blocked: manual fallback. “Find your study JSON, open our approved private link, select that file only, Upload.” Confirm receipt/parse; recording off before payment.
